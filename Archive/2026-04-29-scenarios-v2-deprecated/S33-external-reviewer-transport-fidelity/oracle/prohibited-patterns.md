# Prohibited Patterns

The following patterns are contract breaks for `S33`:

- emitting semantic security findings, QA verdicts, or remediation steps as if the review completed
- changing the assigned `adversarial` review strategy or omitting strategy handling entirely
- rerouting from explicit `codex` selection to `claude` or `gemini`
- using an internal reviewer, QA role, or `$consultant` as a hidden transport fallback
- discussing provider ranking, score-table placement, or semantic review quality
- inventing a launched model or profile beyond `runtime default`
