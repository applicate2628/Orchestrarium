# Prohibited Patterns

These failures should score poorly even if the memo sounds polished.

- generic architecture or product prose with no governing equations, units, invariants, or
  validation criteria
- code, pseudocode, solver implementation, or controller-repair guidance instead of a memo
- security policy, performance policy, or hardware procurement discussion
- treating a Profile-A pass as proof that the full admitted range is validated
- ignoring `E5` uncertainty and limitation notes or pretending they do not matter
- concluding `BLOCKED` even though the packet is sufficient to evaluate the current model
- presenting the work as an algorithm-proof memo or a security constraint package instead of
  computational-scientist validation reasoning
