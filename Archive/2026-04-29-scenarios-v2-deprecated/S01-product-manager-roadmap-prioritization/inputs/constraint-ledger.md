# Constraint Ledger

## Time and launch pressure

- Public evaluator preview target: `2026-06-19`
- The next two roadmap milestones are the last full slots before the preview cutoff.

## Capacity envelope

- The team can absorb only two full roadmap commitments before the preview cutoff.
- At most one small carryalong item can ride along if it does not need its own QA or
  architecture-review attention.

## Gate and quality constraints

- Every new wave must finish `integration -> QA -> architecture-review` before a rerun or
  publication is treated as credible.
- QA and architecture review can each handle only one integrated wave at a time.

## Rerun budget

- There is one clean rerun window in June.
- Spending that window before the remaining semantic gaps are closed means the preview will lock in
  known incompleteness.

## Boundary reminder

- This scenario is not asking for new product discovery.
- This scenario is not asking for implementation planning or execution.
- The artifact must stay one roadmap decision package only.
