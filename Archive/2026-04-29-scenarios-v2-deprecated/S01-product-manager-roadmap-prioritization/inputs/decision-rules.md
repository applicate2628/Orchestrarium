# Decision Rules

- This packet is an owner decision, not a product brief. `S05` exists later for factual
  product-analysis work.
- This packet is not advisory. It must choose a roadmap order rather than list neutral options.
- This packet is not lead routing. Do not add work-item status, resume points, or handoff packets.
- This packet is not implementation. Do not write patches, commands, code blocks, or verifier
  edits.
- Use the initiative titles from `inputs/initiative-cards.md` so the admitted roadmap is explicit.
- The roadmap must name `Priority 1`, `Priority 2`, and the deferred items with re-entry triggers.
- If a required decision cannot be made from the accepted packet, escalate by decision status
  rather than inventing new research or hidden assumptions.
