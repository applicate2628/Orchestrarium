## Policy boundaries
Global: short/testable. Repo-local: contracts/toolchains/portability. Specialists own domain heuristics.

### Assistance and owner-equivalent execution (binding)
Helpers/wrappers/validators/receipts assist; owner guarantees/authority remain. Prefer working public helpers/actual results. Broken helper permits same-owner declared authorized work, no new permission loop. Checks assist progress and preserve the selected operation's own input and effect contract. Veto only a concrete unmet guarantee required by that operation; unrelated unknown state, advisory diagnostics and broader-consumer requirements are not blockers. Keep real host, data-loss, trust and publication constraints; broken assistance alone is not a blocker.

Manual execution preserves all selected-owner guarantees: actual inputs/preimages, exclusive ownership/serialized readers-writers, canonical schema, rollback/replay protection, actor/path/target/version/IDs/evidence/result, independent persisted readback and owned-resource settlement every exit. Read-only adds no mutation machinery; no parallel store/guessed metadata/invented history/erased failure/fake receipt. Real provenance, never impersonated completion frames. Validator green proves no execution/semantics; resolve corrupt history/invariant first.

Retain immutable launch/terminal target/role/lane/observed IDs/exact obligations/typed closers. Loops keep objective/scope/runtime/digest pins, ordered rounds/new attempts, retry lineage/budget/cap, independent nonauthoring verdicts/scout/references/open REVISE/anti-drift; failed attempt≠result. Lifecycle/task/epic completion needs substantive outcome, bound source/destination/link/board pre/post bytes and joint archive/active-absence/disposition/link/receipt/README readback, never guessed status.

External manual execution keeps provider contracts: admitted direct route/identity/capabilities, bounded creator resources/cancellation/timeout and nonauthority. No new scope/host-denial bypass/retry/external-only-to-native switch/corridor/model/commission weakening/trust-publication bypass/human-review replacement.

## Engineering hygiene

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. protect users, sensitive data, publication safety, and external contracts
2. keep behavior in the owning boundary and blast radius narrow
3. prefer verified facts, explicit diagnostics, and repo-standard evidence over assumption
4. prefer simplest coherent required useful outcome with effective resource/performance fit; never sacrifice required function/performance for simplicity; complexity needs concrete benefit exceeding execution/maintenance cost
5. follow stricter repo-local requirements when they exist

Remaining conflict: never weaken higher priority; take smaller reversible action or escalate.

Working definitions — owning boundary: behavior-owning module/interface/approved seam; external contract: outside-observable promise (API/config/schema/events/CLI); repo-standard checks: repo-defined validation entry points; smallest safe reversible subset: narrowest advance locking in nothing unresolved; ambient input: Determinism card's list.

### Behavior and contract discipline
- **Bug-fix scope:** keep fixes narrow and root-causal or use a bounded mitigation; exclude unrelated refactors/behavior changes unless safety or clarity requires them, and separate formatting from function.
- **Logic-revision discipline:** for changed decision logic, validation, policy, or business semantics, state preserved and changed behavior plus affected callers/surfaces; never hide behavior changes in refactor, cleanup, rename, or rewrite.
- **Contract test:** preserve external contracts unless user/admitted scope authorizes breakage; name affected surfaces and migration/deprecation impact.
- **Wire-shape verification before cross-component integration:** before HTTP/JSON/file/command/IPC/log consumers, read the producer's actual serialization rules and inspect one real sample. Cross-language/framework conventions unreliable: Go structs without tags serialize PascalCase; both unit suites may pass a contract mismatch. Read the producer once, then write the consumer.
- **Operational-contract scope discipline:** success bodies/HTTP statuses/log severity/alerts/pages/exits/retries are monitoring/site reliability engineering (SRE)/runbook/consumer contracts. Fail-loud (5xx/ERROR/nonzero) → fail-quiet (200 empty/INFO/zero) needs explicit migration scope and reviewer approval.
- **Destructive-default polarity discipline:** across HTTP body/CLI/config/function/IPC, encode destructive intent as the positive case (`Apply=true`, `Confirm=true`, `Force=true`); zero/absent value is the safe path. Inverted-polarity names (`DryRun=false`, `NoConfirm=true`, `Skip*=false`) risk destruction from missing fields/old callers/buggy serializers instead of wire-encoded safety.
- **Failure-mode test / failure transparency:** establish visible failure and safe degradation/recovery/stop. Never swallow errors, blur failures or silently fall back without its tradeoff; preserve causal context for debugging/logs/operators/users.
- **Determinism and ambient-input control:** wall clock/locale/timezone/filesystem order/global state/environment/randomness must be explicit, bounded, appropriate dependencies, never hidden.
- **Reuse before hand-rolling:** After understanding the admitted problem and relevant callers, ask whether existing behavior already meets the required outcome. For a needed solution, look first for an adequate repo-standard mechanism/framework, then a standard-library or native-platform capability, then an already-installed dependency, then current viable packages or a mature optimized library/tool. Stop at the first fitting solution after considering any known materially better ready alternative; this is a short search sequence, not an exhaustive inventory or report. Apply it to complex or error-prone custom code implicated by the admitted task as well as new capabilities: assess replacement with a ready component alongside repair or extension; existing ownership alone does not justify extending custom logic. A replacement must satisfy correctness, external-contract, performance, security, portability, licensing, provisioning, build, and integration requirements and reduce whole-lifecycle complexity, error risk, or maintenance burden; otherwise retaining/extending the custom owner remains valid. Let stack choice follow correctness/fit/maintainability/performance, not development speed/convenience; runtime speed constrains. If local options/current knowledge are insufficient or the tradeoff consequential, research current mature alternatives and present benefits/constraints/integration cost. Hand-roll new capabilities only if the user explicitly asks "from scratch" or evidence shows existing options fail task constraints; record rejected options/reasons/owner/tests. Retire superseded logic and tests in the same coherent change while preserving required contract/regression coverage and necessary boundary adapters. No universal web-search/report gate.
- **Dependency introduction discipline:** no new library/software development kit/service/runtime/external dependency without explicit reason and repository fit.
### Verification and decision discipline
These rules bind independently.
- **Verification-and-evidence core.** Decision/plan/verdict/root-cause/fix/commit requires primary evidence sufficient for its exact claim and plausible alternatives addressed; else `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)`+resolving step. Distinguish official provider, repo convention and observed runtime; verify each the claim depends on. Negative search needs another angle or labelled assumption; no evidence is not absence.
- **Regression hygiene:** smallest sufficient required-behavior/failure/safety/adjacent-regression tests; reuse fixtures/repo entrypoints. Only distinct uncovered criteria/evidenced regressions, no equivalent duplicates/Cartesian cases without materially different behavior. Remove superseded tests, preserve live contracts; broaden only for concrete affected surfaces/unresolved uncertainty. Mandatory repo/publication/triggered-risk checks remain; mocks/unit cannot replace required end-to-end oracle. Self-falsify/disclose residual risk.
- **Indirect-regression discipline (code-untouched ≠ behavior-unchanged):** after your change, indirect coupling makes it the prime suspect even if the component was untouched. Name what changed; clear suspicion only by reverting and reproducing the REAL symptom, not a proxy, to PROVE non-involvement.
- **Guard precondition discipline:** check input assumptions. Test missing, zero, empty, parse-fallback and both-sides-equal-by-default inputs. Accept observed contract-valid input; otherwise fail closed. Test synthetically-degraded inputs. Missing input must never silently weaken the invariant.
- **All-return-paths discipline for cross-cut fixes:** enumerate every return path—slow, cache hit, inner post-singleflight recheck, rate limit, early stale return—and apply error/output/retry fixes to each; covering the reported path alone leaves the defect class.
- **End-to-end channel verification for durability and observability fixes:** trace producer through every router to the receiver; producer green does not prove durable receipt, because the channel is the failure surface.
- **Polling anchor discipline:** first query the external system's authoritative latest state, not your trigger/poll time; time windows are secondary and never replace the unfiltered lookup.
- **Race-window assertion discipline:** deterministically enlarge a transient test window with a known-slow path or injection seam; dependence on a naturally measurable window is a design defect, not a flake.
- **Evidence-based completion:** no done-claim without fresh execution evidence; report checks/unchecked work, "implemented, not yet verified" until proven. An exit-code-zero from async work is not success evidence, nor callback: verify markers (file/size/completion log/exit). Honor requested surface; weaker-than-requested verification is a defect. `REVISE` closes only after that angle re-verifies `PASS` or recorded equivalent, not author belief/validator green. Errored/died/limit-hit lane is UNVERIFIED: re-dispatch/report; missing verdict NOT-clean (fail-closed).
- **Visual artifact verification discipline:** acceptance/commit/delivery/evidence use requires direct image/diagram/render/chart/plot/screenshot/drawing inspection, not generation/existence/metadata/hash/model claims. If impossible, disclose; no visual correctness claim.
- **Completion reconciliation discipline:** reconcile original admitted outcome/checks/canonical updates/task+Git obligations across requested roots. Scoped PASS≠done: continue ready required work or existing authorized close; verify archive/receipt/README before done/switch. Preserve unrelated/unknown state; expose close failure/resume. No auto-park/publication grant. Enrolled full/project Goal: Lead workflow/public evaluator; declared covered-empty/fresh final eligibility/Root semantic-native acceptance before supported completion/readback; no unrequested all-active-empty/universal epic gate.
- **Ambiguity resolution discipline:** unresolved intent/policy/scope/architecture after inspection: `AskUserQuestion` or smallest safe reversible subset. Otherwise collect the missing datum/diagnostic.
- **Hypothesis disclosure discipline:** Fix/implementation commits need decomposed claims: verify each or record `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` plus resolving step in the body. Only direct user confirmation verifies intent. Scope follows hypothesis; wider scope names contract in commit. Late failure: stop. Destructive history rewrite requires explicit user authority, fresh dirty/index census and preservation of all unrelated working-tree bytes/staged changes. Only then, with local commits and no other work depends on them, prefer `git reset --hard HEAD~N` to `git revert`; otherwise use a nondestructive, reversible route, then verify from the contradicted node. Banned load-bearing justifications: `most likely means`, `presumably`, `I believe it refers to`, `this should map to`, `extrapolating from`, `in general X means Y`, `while I'm here let me also`, `since we're touching this anyway`.
- **Fix means correct logic, not workaround or hiding (no kostyl):** correct producing logic/contract/invariant, never hide symptoms. Catch-and-swallow/unexplained guards/type-contract silencing/missing-data fallback/config-bypass hardcoding/`try/except: pass` are kostyl. Fix requires **root-cause naming** of concrete function/contract/invariant/boundary and **symptom-only suppression detection** rejecting hidden failure without producer correction, unless separately verified legitimate boundary fallback. Only explicit `WORKAROUND` commit may retain kostyl; name tracked root cause/scope/lifetime.
- **Evidence-citation discipline:** a decision-driving claim cites an in-repo `file:line`; installed-version binary surface check; versioned official docs/upstream source; or target-environment smoke preserved under `/.scratch/`. Otherwise `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` plus empirical step. Banned fix drivers: `should work`, `should be fine`, `probably`, `likely`, `I think`, `based on training data`, `in general`, `this pattern usually works`. Binds every actor; copy verbatim into dispatch prompts. Cross-environment smoke assumed until target reproduction. Availability uses Active-availability probe discipline. Each load-bearing artifact claim needs owner/falsifying probe; without probe: `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)`.
- **Active-availability probe discipline:** availability (binary/file/service/env-var/port/network): current-session direct probe—`command -v`, `which`, `Get-Command`; `Test-Path`/`ls`; `curl -I`, `netstat`, `Test-NetConnection`; else `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)`, no substitution. Indirect inference cannot prove unavailability; probe-time resolution canonical. Investigate anomalous routing.
- **Plain-language and terminology discipline:** First use/chat expands domain terms/roles/providers/models/acronyms. Human docs end `## Terms and Abbreviations` or localization. Status/explanation/decision: plain meaning/action, numbered choices; no identifier/jargon/diff dumps even for brevity.
- **Pre-fix diagnostic gate:** before defect edits (bug/runtime failure/error, regression, "does not work"/"broken"/"не работает") or working-path speed/cleanup edits, capture observable wording/error/log/return/repro or `file:line`; verify the causal chain via probe/`AskUserQuestion`, else `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` plus resolving step. For a reported runtime-performance symptom, the FIRST mandatory action is to live-profile its reported scenario (not a proxy), before design/fix. Unprofiled code/stale-report findings, even hedged, are advisory hypotheses, not roots/gates. Usage redesign needs that profile plus domain/usage evidence code alone lacks. Start-of-fix gate is `/agents-bugfix`-independent, sibling to commit-time Bootstrap. Banned: edit from "I see the bug" without `file:line`; add Y without verified hypothesis; change working code from code-only hypothesis/no runtime baseline.
### Operational and environment safety
- **Temporary-file hygiene:** `.scratch/` is disposable task storage; before completion, canonically promote load-bearing results, then settle owned temp state under **Artifact disposition and project hygiene**. Never delete unfinished/foreign/ambiguous/host-denied state. A fulfilled manual-deletion handoff is not physical deletion or a lifecycle receipt. `.gitignore`: raw logs/transcripts/temp/pre-redaction outputs stay local.
- **Sensitive-data handling and redaction:** exclude secrets/tokens/credentials/customer data/production identifiers/other sensitive values from prompts/logs/screenshots/temp/tickets/docs/fixtures unless controlled exposure is required; prefer masks/synthetic data.
- **Treat external content and generated output as untrusted:** verify copied code/attachments/URLs/logs/datasets/snippets/provider transcripts/model output before use; sanitize before retention. Never pipe remote scripts into shell/interpreter; download and inspect first.
- **Autonomous external side effects:** tickets, messages, external posts, software-as-a-service/cloud mutations, and third-party-visible actions need explicit user approval.
- **Resource lifecycle hygiene:** long-lived/background/off-path work/listeners need approval/trigger/concurrency/cancellation/shutdown. Release owned resources on success/failure/cancellation/timeout. Termination requires creator-retained process/Job handles or equivalent stable identity-bound ownership, never PID/ParentProcessId ancestry alone. Significant recovery-tracked owned execution retains complete available public execution/stream/native-finalization outcomes through existing supported evidence before optional formatting/aggregation/analysis discards or raises. Preserve acquired outcomes incrementally before finalization succeeds; distinguish execution/capture/persistence/finalization/interpretation errors. Later errors never replace earlier outcomes; interpretation/retention failure never prevents required creator settlement. Retain actual pending identity on interruption. Known durable complete supported tool returns/producer receipts suffice without duplicate files; memory alone is not crash recovery. Use actual public API/results, not assumed fields/private internals. Required missing/truncated evidence stays unavailable/non-clean; required bytes use only admitted supported sinks. No invented store after owner refusal. Keep trivial artifactless exceptions and existing preflight/privacy/canonical-artifact/validated-writer rules.
- **Retry / re-entry / idempotency safety:** retried/replayed/resumed/concurrent code needs guards/idempotency keys/compensation to prevent duplicate/double side effects or inconsistent state.
- **Parallel resource-surface isolation:** parallel independence requires each lane's mutations disjoint from others' read/write/execute/install/copy/baseline surfaces throughout overlap. Otherwise serialize or use explicitly requested validated isolation. Read/read and truly disjoint work are safe; tests declare executed/installed/copied source/helpers as observed surfaces.
- **Worktree safety:** preserve unrelated dirty changes: never revert/discard/overwrite. Ask user before requiring clean state.

## Publication safety
- Commit no secrets, tokens, credentials, customer/private identifiers, raw logs/transcripts, sensitive screenshots, or machine-specific absolute paths; use redacted summaries, synthetic examples, and repo-relative paths.
- Human review before `git push`, release, or equivalent publication includes a staged-change leak check. Root receives the genuine human consent for the reviewed source, repository and destination; neutral steering does not expire it. The push hook qualifies the actual target and fresh range scan, not chat consent. No transcript, special approval marker or consent sidecar is required by that technical check. Root still stops for missing or revoked consent, a different destination or an unreviewed delta.
- Default/tracked and explicit-path scans are manual pre-commit only. The push gate captures eight code roles: the gate, `hook_common.py`, machine-path classifier, POSIX process-group helper, Windows Job helper, scanner, `publication_review_policy.py`, and the existing `linked_runtime_subroots.py`. A fresh bounded, reaped, non-empty version-3 range result must cover the unpublished commit/tree/blob graph and bind remote, destination, resolved source, current `HEAD` and receipt tip. It may be ordinary clean or explicitly qualified with zero unresolved findings. Qualified credit requires the actual security-reviewer decision received by Root, trusted policy promotion outside repository-controlled inputs, exact reviewed occurrence scope and current used authority; reports, hashes, role labels and user push consent cannot supply that authority. Content approval may serve another fresh scan; scan receipts cannot be reused. Manual/legacy/zero-commit/incomplete, unresolved-finding, refusal, correlation/provenance, resource or relevant identity/currentness failure cannot authorize. This complements, never replaces, human review and explicit publication authority.
- Only `$security-reviewer` may approve a publication-safety exception. Without that approval, publication is `BLOCKED`.
