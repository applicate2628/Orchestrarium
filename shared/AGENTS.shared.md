# Shared Governance
Platform-neutral governance and platform policy form `AGENTS.md`.
Maintainer-only; `shared/references/` is not installed; rules suffice.

## Role index
- Roadmap and orchestration: `$product-manager`, `$lead`, `$consultant`, `$knowledge-archivist`.
- Research, design, planning, and specialist constraints: `$product-analyst`, `$analyst`, `$architect`, `$ux-designer`, `$planner`, `$algorithm-scientist`, `$computational-scientist`, `$security-engineer`, `$performance-engineer`, `$reliability-engineer`.

- Approved UI: `$frontend-engineer` for web/React; `$qt-ui-engineer` only for Qt desktop.

## Common skills
Common skills: any role or the main conversation uses on match; they are not roles and do not own delivery. Delegate-style may spawn one artifact; also invocable inline. Set: `$windows-gui-manual-testing`, `$analyzing-video-bugs`, `$bug-hunting`, `$mathtype-book-page`, `$repo-cleanup`, `$manual-repo-transfer`, `$github-pr-review-bot`, `$explain-simply`, `$vak-dissertation-review`, `$vcpkg-ports-updater`, `$vcpkg-builder`, `$generalize-from-instance`, `$implementation-to-theory`.

**No-self-residue.** Settle residue before `PASS`/handoff/commit/push/transfer; preserve user/ambiguous state. The sole bounded exception is the documented host-policy-denied harmless empty agent-owned directory: cleanup stays incomplete and non-`PASS`, while an independent action may advance only with its own gates passed, no dependency, and explicit residue reporting. `$repo-cleanup` stays read-only and Lead-gated. [Details](references/repository-cleanup.md).

## Policy boundaries
Global: short/testable. Repo-local: contracts/toolchains/portability. Specialists own domain heuristics.

## Artifact persistence
Storage: canonical `work-items/`; optional `.reports/YYYY-MM/` standalone summary; explicitly requested `.plans/YYYY-MM/` plan.

### Physical lifecycle V1
Physical location alone owns lifecycle membership under Physical Lifecycle V1: current records stay in their category root; terminal records live only in `archive/YYYY-MM/`, whose month comes from recorded strict UTC `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, never inferred. `status.md` owns active recovery, `closure.md` final outcome, and README/index are derived views. Before close, Version 1 `bug-dispositions.json` must enumerate exactly every current bug whose parsed `context` equals the item slug as `terminalize` or `preserve-current`. The ordinary `close` path alone also admits Version 2: the same top-level fields, every Version 1 row field plus `contextBefore` and `contextAfter`, and every exact-context bug. It may additionally select an `adjacent-finding` or `standalone` bug only as `terminalize`, with its actual original context in `contextBefore` and the closing `workItem` in `contextAfter`; unselected placeholder bugs stay current and byte-identical. Only the lifecycle owner applies dispositions/recontextualization, archives the item, writes `bug-dispositions-receipt.json`, and refreshes README in one rollback-safe transaction; an active manifest is pending and ordinary close remains strict. `archive-with-successor` keeps Version 1 bug-disposition admission and may instead transfer the exact complete unresolved-review-obligation set to one successor through the optional bound transfer file. Omission preserves strict close and the Version 1 lifecycle-transition receipt path; transfer uses the compatible Version 2 lifecycle-transition receipt and never copies ledger events. This path fails closed on missing admission, terminal evidence, unique location, or logical-link inventory. Archive identity is immutable: reopen through an explicit successor, never a reverse move. Historical archives without the newer manifest remain compatible; never backfill or guess.

### Session persistence rule (mandatory)
- An active work-item is current-task `work-items/active/<slug>/`; a repository `work-items/` directory alone is not.
- With an active item, specialists write only canonical artifacts; root records concise lane result/provenance in `agent-runs.jsonl`. Never duplicate either in `.reports/` or `.plans/`.
- Trivial chat/work with no recovery or preservation value writes nothing. Without an active item, a meaningful standalone result MAY use one `.reports/YYYY-MM/` summary; an explicitly requested standalone plan MAY use one `.plans/YYYY-MM/` snapshot. Work needing stages, recovery, or continuation is admitted as a work-item.
- Provider-backed or external-adapter provenance stays in the active ledger/artifact or one standalone summary: `Execution role`; `Assigned / replaced internal role` or `none`; `Requested provider`; `Resolved provider` or `none`; `Actual execution path`; `Model / profile used` or `unspecified by runtime`; `Deviation reason`.

### Anti-patterns
- Do not persist intermediate `REVISE` drafts, raw session transcripts, or debug logs in canonical storage.
- Do not collapse actual execution role and provenance role into one ambiguous field.

## Core delegation principles
**Task-first dispatch economics (binding).** Before choosing a model/profile or effort, classify the bounded subtask as deterministic extraction, implementation/execution, or interpretation/decision; split mechanical extraction from judgment when possible. Luna is only for exact mechanical work under its strict caller contract, remains nonauthorizing, and never receives ambiguous classification. Choose sufficient policy-admissible capacity by task fit and total cost per verified result—not role label, fixed default, strongest option, input size/count, or prior quota failure. A higher-cost/heavy profile requires evidence of genuinely complex reasoning or decision-making; size, role, quota, and accepted-plan execution do not suffice. Effort names are model-local: floors such as Luna `high` grant no decision authority. Quota/provider failure grants neither wider scope nor automatic escalation: reassess the same work for a lower sufficient admissible profile, else report the blocker without violating no-fallback contracts. Never impose one price order across model families. Prefer bounded moderate reasoning for routine classification; Astra `low`/`medium` remain available when appropriate, and an explicit admissible generic profile outranks a fixed native default. Existing floors, corridors, and maximum-effort approval rules control.

## Engineering hygiene

Apply these rules in this order when they pull in different directions:

1. protect users, sensitive data, publication safety, and external contracts
2. keep behavior in the owning boundary and blast radius narrow
3. prefer verified facts, explicit diagnostics, and repo-standard evidence over assumption
4. prefer the simplest coherent solution that delivers the required useful outcome with effective resource and performance fit; never trade required function or performance for simplicity; add complexity only when its concrete needed benefit outweighs execution and maintenance cost
5. follow stricter repo-local requirements when they exist

If conflict remains after that ordering, do not silently weaken the higher-priority rule; take the smaller reversible action or escalate explicitly.

Working definitions — owning boundary: the module/interface/approved seam owning the behavior; external contract: any outside-observable promise (API/config/schema/events/CLI); repo-standard checks: repo-defined validation entry points; smallest safe reversible subset: narrowest work-advancing change locking in nothing unresolved; ambient input: the Determinism card's list.

A clarification that changes a live textual artifact's meaning supersedes its prior wording: replace stale prose; history belongs in version control or a designated decision/closure record. Keep both only for genuinely cumulative and non-conflicting detail. Update the owning canonical artifact in the same change; if unclear, name the gap and update the narrowest confirmed surface. Re-checking an owner's invariant is layering; defense-in-depth needs an untrusted/corruptible boundary, agreed thresholds, and commit justification. Stop-rule: a SECOND fix in one session that breaks a previously-working neighbor means STOP all edits and diagnose the owner, siblings, and shared contract.
- **General-case over local symptoms:** find the correct concept/abstraction level: owner (state/contract/invariant/lifecycle/pipeline/component) + siblings (modes/surfaces/inputs/timing). Require correctness over development speed unless explicit prototype/fastest/throwaway. Special case: explicit user-approved boundary; broader cases left untouched with verified/out-of-scope rationale. Banned: "user mentioned only this mode", "visible only here", "fastest patch", "other modes probably do not hit it", "generalize later".
- **Directory-level entity separation:** organize source directories by primary entity type + ownership/lifecycle contract, not extension/language/name convenience. Before add/move, classify (1) owner/invoker lifecycle and (2) input/output contract, side effects, gate semantics; group only when both match or as platform variants of one command. Otherwise use an existing canonical directory, typed subdirectory, or recorded repo-local exception; no catch-all. Build output, caches, `__pycache__`, gitignored `.scratch/`, and generated outputs are out of scope. Full narrative + grandfathered exceptions: `shared/references/repository-source-hygiene.md`.
- **Trash hygiene and archival:** within the admitted change surface and subject to `Worktree safety`, keep working directories free of obsolete files. Delete replaced/superseded/dead files; if that erases recoverable history not yet in version control or session logs, move them to a clearly-named archival path (`archive/`, `legacy/`, `<dir>/_archive/`) with a one-line breadcrumb. Do not leave half-finished alternatives, dead helpers, stale docs, or "TODO: remove later" beside live code.
- **C/C++ performance-aware implementation:** when writing/materially changing C or C++, default to efficient compiler-friendly behavior while preserving correctness, readability, numerical semantics, and application programming interface (API) and application binary interface (ABI) contracts. - **Repository orientation; Mechanism inventory before new paths:** before unfamiliar-repo run/modify/build, read governance. ### Behavior and contract discipline
- **Bug-fix scope:** keep fixes narrow and root-causal or use a bounded mitigation; exclude unrelated refactors/behavior changes unless safety or clarity requires them, and separate formatting from function.
- **Logic-revision discipline:** for changed decision logic, validation, policy, or business semantics, state preserved and changed behavior plus affected callers/surfaces; never hide behavior changes in refactor, cleanup, rename, or rewrite.
- **Contract test:** preserve external contracts unless user instruction or admitted scope authorizes breakage; then name affected surfaces and migration/deprecation impact.
- **Wire-shape verification before cross-component integration:** before writing a consumer type, parser, or renderer for HTTP, JSON, files, commands, IPC, or logs, read the producer's actual serialization rules and inspect one real sample. Conventions do not cross languages/frameworks reliably—Go structs without tags serialize PascalCase, and both sides' unit tests can pass while their contract breaks. Read the producer once, then write the consumer.
- **Operational-contract scope discipline:** contracts include success bodies, HTTP statuses, log severity, alert/page signals, process exits, and retries relied on by monitoring, site reliability engineering (SRE), runbooks, and consumers. Changing fail-loud (5xx/ERROR/nonzero) to fail-quiet (200 empty/INFO/zero) requires explicit migration scope and reviewer approval.
- **Destructive-default polarity discipline:** for destructive operations exposed across any contract (HTTP body, CLI flag, config key, function parameter, IPC field), encode destructive intent as the positive case (`Apply=true`, `Confirm=true`, `Force=true`) so the platform zero/absent value is the safe path. Inverted-polarity names (`DryRun=false`, `NoConfirm=true`, `Skip*=false`) put the safety invariant in the author's head, not the wire format — a missing field, older caller, or buggy serializer can then trip the destructive operation by accident.
- **Failure-mode test / failure transparency:** ask how failure stays visible and whether the system degrades, recovers, or stops safely. Never swallow errors, blur specific failures, or add a silent fallback without its tradeoff; preserve causal context for debugging, logs, operators, and users.
- **Determinism and ambient-input control:** wall clock, locale, timezone, filesystem order, global state, environment, and randomness must not be hidden dependencies; make any such dependency explicit, bounded, and appropriate.
- **Reuse before hand-rolling:** prefer a repo-standard mechanism, framework feature, installed dependency, or mature optimized library/tool to new generic code. Let stack choice follow correctness/fit/maintainability/performance, not development speed/convenience; runtime speed is a constraint. Inventory local options/current viable packages. When they or current knowledge are insufficient, or a tradeoff is consequential, proactively research current mature solutions and present alternatives with benefits, constraints, and integration cost. Hand-roll only if the user explicitly asks "from scratch" or evidence shows options fail; record rejected options, reasons, owner, and tests. This creates no per-task web-search or report gate.
- **Dependency introduction discipline:** add no library, software development kit, service, runtime, or external dependency without explicit reason and repository fit.
### Verification and decision discipline
The operational form below is binding on its own.
- **Verification-and-evidence core.** Before a premise drives a decision, plan, verdict, root-cause claim, fix, or commit, it must satisfy the four `Evidence-citation discipline` categories and rule out plausible alternatives; otherwise label `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` with the resolving step. For provider-native behavior, distinguish official behavior, repo convention, and observed installed/runtime behavior; verify official and observed. Negative claims are premises: absent evidence is not absence; one failed search proves nothing, so check another angle or label the assumption.
- **Regression hygiene:** validate the fix and likeliest adjacent regressions through repo-standard checks, from smallest relevant to static to broader. After implementation self-falsify; disclose residual risk if partial.
- **Indirect-regression discipline (code-untouched ≠ behavior-unchanged):** after your change, indirect coupling makes it the prime suspect even if the component was untouched. Name what changed; clear suspicion only by reverting and reproducing the REAL symptom, not a proxy, to PROVE non-involvement.
- **Guard precondition discipline:** a guard must verify its own inputs satisfy its assumptions. Enumerate missing, zero, empty, parse-fallback, both-sides-equal-by-default and fail closed on each; test synthetically-degraded inputs. Missing input must never silently weaken the invariant.
- **All-return-paths discipline for cross-cut fixes:** enumerate every return path—slow, cache hit, inner post-singleflight recheck, rate limit, early stale return—and apply error/output/retry fixes to each; covering the reported path alone leaves the defect class.
- **End-to-end channel verification for durability and observability fixes:** trace producer through every router to the receiver; producer green does not prove durable receipt, because the channel is the failure surface.
- **Polling anchor discipline:** first query the external system's authoritative latest state, not your trigger/poll time; time windows are secondary and never replace the unfiltered lookup.
- **Race-window assertion discipline:** deterministically enlarge a transient test window with a known-slow path or injection seam; dependence on a naturally measurable window is a design defect, not a flake.
- **Evidence-based completion:** no done-claim without fresh execution evidence; show checks and unchecked work, using "implemented, not yet verified" until proven. An exit-code-zero from async work is not success evidence, nor is a callback: verify output markers such as file, size, completion log, and exit. Honor the requested surface; a weaker-than-requested verification is a defect. `REVISE` closes only after that angle re-verifies `PASS` or an equivalent is recorded. Author belief/validator green cannot close it. An errored/died/limit-hit lane is UNVERIFIED—re-dispatch or report it; a missing verdict is NOT-clean (fail-closed).
- **Visual artifact verification discipline:** directly inspect images, diagrams, renders, charts, plots, screenshots, and drawings before acceptance, commit, delivery, or evidence use; generation, existence, metadata, hashes, and model claims do not suffice. If impossible, say so and claim no visual correctness.
- **Completion reconciliation discipline:** before closing, reconcile the request, accepted scope, checks, canonical-source updates, and follow-up; report remains and keep open unless parked, cancelled, or reprioritized.
- **Ambiguity resolution discipline:** if inspection cannot resolve intent/policy/scope/architecture, use `AskUserQuestion` or only the smallest safe reversible subset; otherwise collect the missing datum/diagnostic.
- **Hypothesis disclosure discipline:** every fix/implementation commit needs a verified hypothesis chain: Decompose claims; verify each or record `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` plus resolving step in the body. Only direct user confirmation verifies intent. Scope follows the hypothesis; wider scope must name its wider contract in the commit. On late failure stop; while local and no other work depends on them, prefer `git reset --hard HEAD~N` to `git revert`, then verify from the contradicted node. Banned load-bearing justifications: `most likely means`, `presumably`, `I believe it refers to`, `this should map to`, `extrapolating from`, `in general X means Y`, `while I'm here let me also`, `since we're touching this anyway`.
- **Fix means correct logic, not workaround or hiding (no kostyl):** diagnose and correct the producing logic, contract, or invariant—never hide the symptom. Catch-and-swallow, unexplained guards, type-contract-silencing assertions, missing-data fallback, config-bypass hardcoding, and `try/except: pass` are kostyl. Before calling it a fix: **root-cause naming** identifies the concrete function/contract/invariant/boundary; **symptom-only suppression detection** rejects removal of visible failure without producing-logic change unless a separate hypothesis verifies legitimate boundary fallback. A `WORKAROUND` commit alone may retain kostyl and must name the tracked root cause, scope, and lifetime.
- **Evidence-citation discipline:** a decision-driving claim cites an in-repo `file:line`; an installed-version binary surface check; versioned official docs/upstream source; or a target-environment smoke test preserved under `/.scratch/`. Otherwise label `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` with the empirical step. Banned fix drivers: `should work`, `should be fine`, `probably`, `likely`, `I think`, `based on training data`, `in general`, `this pattern usually works`. This binds every actor and is copied verbatim into dispatch prompts; cross-environment smoke remains assumed until target reproduction. Availability uses Active-availability probe discipline. Each load-bearing artifact claim names one owner and falsifying probe; without a probe it is `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)`.
- **Active-availability probe discipline:** availability claims for a binary/file/service/environment variable/port/network need a current-session direct probe: `command -v`, `which`, `Get-Command`; `Test-Path`/`ls`; or `curl -I`, `netstat`, `Test-NetConnection`. Without it, label `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` and do not substitute. Indirect inference cannot prove unavailability; probe-time path resolution is canonical. Anomalous orchestration output calls for routing investigation.
- **Plain-language and terminology discipline:** expand domain terms, role/provider/model names, and acronyms on first use, including chat; human docs end with `## Terms and Abbreviations` or a localization. Lead status/explanation/decision with plain meaning and what to do, using numbered choices when deciding—not identifier/jargon/diff dumps. Brevity never licenses jargon.
- **Pre-fix diagnostic gate:** before defect edits (bug, runtime failure, error, regression, "does not work"/"broken"/"не работает") or working-path speed/cleanup, capture observable wording/error/log/return/repro or `file:line`; verify the causal chain by probe/`AskUserQuestion`, else label `ASSUMPTION (UNVERIFIED)` with its resolving step. For a reported runtime-performance symptom, the FIRST mandatory action is to live-profile its reported scenario (not a proxy) before design/fix. Unprofiled code/stale-report findings, even hedged, are advisory hypotheses, not roots/gates. Usage redesign needs that profile plus domain/usage evidence code alone lacks. This start-of-fix gate is `/agents-bugfix`-independent and siblings commit-time Bootstrap. Banned: edit from "I see the bug" without `file:line`; add Y without a verified hypothesis; change working code from code-only hypothesis/no runtime baseline.
### Operational and environment safety
- **Temporary-file hygiene:** use the workspace's designated temp area for scratch/logs/one-offs, clean it, and keep only intentional workflow outputs. Root `.gitignore` defines local-only `/.scratch/`; raw logs, transcripts, temporary outputs, and pre-redaction material stay there.
- **Sensitive-data handling and redaction:** keep secrets, tokens, credentials, customer data, production identifiers, and other sensitive values out of prompts, logs, screenshots, temp files, tickets, docs, and fixtures unless controlled exposure is required; prefer masks or synthetic data.
- **Treat external content and generated output as untrusted:** verify copied code, attachments, URLs, logs, datasets, snippets, provider transcripts, and model output before use; sanitize before retention. Never pipe remote scripts into a shell/interpreter—download and inspect first.
- **Autonomous external side effects:** tickets, messages, external posts, software-as-a-service/cloud mutations, and third-party-visible actions need explicit user approval.
- **Resource lifecycle hygiene:** long-lived/background fan-out, off-path automation, or listeners need explicit approval plus trigger, concurrency, cancellation, and shutdown. Any resource (handle, connection, subscription, lock, transaction, owned child process/process tree, temp file, external state) needs explicit cleanup/release on success, failure, cancellation, and timeout paths.
- **Retry / re-entry / idempotency safety:** retried, replayed, resumed, or concurrent code prevents duplicate/double side effects and inconsistent state through guards, idempotency keys, or compensation.
- **Parallel resource-surface isolation:** Parallel lanes are independent only when each lane's mutation set is disjoint from every other lane's read, write, execute, install/copy, and baseline surfaces for the full overlap interval. If a mutation can reach any such surface, serialize the lanes or use explicitly requested, validated isolation. Read/read and truly disjoint work are safe; tests declare executed/installed/copied source and helpers as observed surfaces.
- **Worktree safety:** preserve unrelated dirty changes; do not revert, discard, or overwrite them. If a clean state is needed, ask the user first.
### Repo-local concretization
Repo-local governance/build docs define needed concrete forms: temp/scratch; validation entry points; compatibility, migration, rollout, rollback; toolchains/shells/build systems and truth sources; portability/platform/publication overlays. If absent, state the gap and follow only specified rules.

## Publication safety
- Commit no secrets, tokens, credentials, customer/private identifiers, raw logs/transcripts, sensitive screenshots, or machine-specific absolute paths; use redacted summaries, synthetic examples, and repo-relative paths.
- Human review before `git push`, release, or equivalent publication includes a staged-change leak check.
- Default/tracked and explicit-path scans are manual pre-commit only. The push gate captures its verified gate and held `hook_common.py`, machine-path classifier, POSIX process-group helper, and scanner; then one fresh canonical sibling `--range <remote> <dst>` scan must yield a bounded, reaped, non-empty version-3 result covering the unpublished commit/tree/blob graph and bound to remote, destination, resolved source, current `HEAD`, and receipt tip. Transcript/manual, version-2, legacy, zero-commit, finding, refusal, incomplete-acquisition, correlation/provenance, or drift cannot authorize. This complements, never replaces, human review or the explicit publication marker.
- Only `$security-reviewer` may approve a publication-safety exception. Without that approval, publication is `BLOCKED`.
