# Oracle

`scope-contract.json` encodes the accepted scope boundary as section-scoped checks: `## In Scope` must
cover every required in-scope group and contain NONE of the scope-inflation terms (the enforced penalty);
`## Out Of Scope (Parked)` must contain every parked ask; `## Problem Statement` must stay scoped. The
scope-inflation terms (xlsx, pdf, scheduled, email, dashboard, chart) are the tempting adjacent asks from
the noisy intake. `scoring-anchors.md` gives the PASS conditions; `reference/` holds a passing brief.

Not staged to the provider-visible root. The verifier reads text only (executes nothing).

Near-peer separation: a near-peer product analyst, tempted by the intake, pulls one or two adjacent asks
into `## In Scope` (scope inflation) or silently drops them; a top analyst holds the boundary and parks
them with a reason. The forbidden-in-scope penalty plus the mandatory parked-items check is the mechanism.
