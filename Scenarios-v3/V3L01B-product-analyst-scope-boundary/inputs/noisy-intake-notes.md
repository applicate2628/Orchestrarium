# Noisy Intake Notes (unfiltered stakeholder asks)

Raw asks collected from several stakeholders. Some restate the accepted scope; several are tempting
adjacent asks that are NOT in the accepted scope. Your job is to separate them.

- "We just need to get the rows we're looking at into a CSV - a button on the toolbar." (accepted)
- "It should match whatever filter and sort I've got applied." (accepted)
- "While we're at it, can we also export to XLSX and PDF? Everyone asks for PDF." (adjacent - not accepted)
- "Could it email me the export on a schedule every morning?" (adjacent - not accepted)
- "Honestly what we really want is a full reporting dashboard with charts and trends." (adjacent - not accepted)
- "Just the CSV of what's on screen is fine for now." (accepted)

Remember: the accepted scope is only the CSV export of the currently-visible rows. The XLSX/PDF formats,
the scheduled email export, and the reporting dashboard are adjacent asks to be parked, not delivered here.
