# Bounded Scope (accepted)

The accepted, funded problem for this brief is exactly:

- Add a CSV export of the currently-visible result rows to the existing results table.
- The export is triggered by a download button in the results toolbar.
- The export reflects the current filter and sort of the table.

That is the whole accepted scope. Anything beyond CSV-of-visible-rows is a separate effort and belongs
in the parked out-of-scope section, not in this brief's in-scope list.
