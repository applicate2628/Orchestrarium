  v2 benchmark runs.
- Reviewers do not want provider caveats hidden inside one global summary paragraph.
- Product wants milestone 1 to stay role-complete even if provider reruns happen later.

  is not part of the accepted brief.

  defined.
- There is pressure to ship a draft quickly, but the design must not collapse semantic roles into
  pack summaries again.
- We do not yet have a final answer on whether near-ties should share rank or trigger a follow-up
  scenario.
