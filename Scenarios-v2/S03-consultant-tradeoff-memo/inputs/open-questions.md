# Open Questions

1. How much section overlap will there really be between consultant, analyst, and
   algorithm-scientist memo bundles?
2. Is the current evidence strong enough to justify a bundle-local pilot, or is the reuse risk
   already high enough to merit shared scaffolding first?
3. Does waiting for more evidence reduce risk, or does it mainly postpone the first concrete signal
   about memo-bundle ergonomics?
4. One stakeholder suggested turning the consultant memo into the authoritative next-step route. Is
   that a reasonable shortcut or a role-drift trap?

These questions are intentionally unresolved. A strong consultant memo names what is still unknown
instead of pretending the packet is complete.
