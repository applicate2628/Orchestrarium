# Scoring Anchors

These anchors translate the shared `owner, advisory, factual, design, planning` profile into
`S03`-specific reads.

## Strong pass signals

- recommends `Option A - one local advisory pilot first`
- stays explicitly advisory-only and non-blocking
- compares both `Option B` and `Option C` instead of pretending there is no tradeoff
- grounds the recommendation in the accepted brief, evidence summary, and open questions
- names concrete risks, assumptions, uncertainty, and confidence without drifting into a plan
- ends with a reusable continuation prompt for the lead

## Partial-pass signals

- chooses the preferred direction but leaves one major tradeoff or uncertainty implicit
- stays advisory-only but drifts into light planning language
- compares alternatives but weakens the memo's evidence grounding or continuation prompt

## Failure signals

- takes routing or approval authority away from the lead
- writes implementation tasks, phase sequencing, or architecture ownership instead of a memo
- treats missing evidence as a reason to claim certainty or to avoid any recommendation at all
- turns the scenario into transport or provider analysis rather than consultant judgment
