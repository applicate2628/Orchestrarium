# Scoring Anchors

## Strong brief

- introduces one coherent hierarchy ladder shared across desktop and web
- distinguishes primary, secondary, and deferred information without ambiguity
- explains how blockers, reviewer asks, and evidence freshness should visually compete with routine
  metadata
- keeps the artifact UX-focused and avoids implementation or review drift
- makes cross-surface naming and grouping consistent enough to reduce re-scanning

## Partial brief

- improves hierarchy on one surface but leaves the other surface vague
- identifies important facts but does not clearly rank them
- suggests better grouping yet drifts into styling notes or light implementation hints

## Weak brief

- treats the problem as mere color tuning or copy cleanup
- offers a bug list, audit report, or implementation plan instead of a structure brief
- leaves primary versus secondary information unresolved
- ignores cross-surface naming drift and information-structure mismatch
