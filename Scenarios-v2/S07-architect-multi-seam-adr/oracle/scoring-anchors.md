# Scoring Anchors

These anchors translate the shared `owner, advisory, factual, design, planning` profile into
`S07`-specific reads.

## Strong pass signals

- chooses `Option B - bundle-local oracle and verifier seam`
- grounds the decision in the accepted brief and factual memo instead of inventing new requirements
- explains why `scenario.yaml` and the shared scorer are protected global boundaries
- defines the approved extension seam, dependency direction, contracts, and test strategy clearly
- includes a numbered claims section that an architecture reviewer can falsify later

## Partial-pass signals

- identifies the real tradeoffs but leaves one boundary or contract implicit
- chooses the right seam but writes a noisy or partly planner-shaped package
- preserves the protected surfaces but weakens the claims section or observability expectations

## Failure signals

- reopens the universal metadata contract without escalation
- moves scenario-local architect rules into the central scorer
- writes implementation code, patch steps, or a phase plan instead of a design package
- ignores the accepted score-profile and bundle-self-containment constraints
