# Scoring Anchors

These anchors translate the shared `owner, advisory, factual, design, planning` profile into
`S01`-specific reads.

## Strong pass signals

- commits to `Wave 4 materialization and gates` as `Priority 1`
- commits to `Final coverage wave and rerun readiness` as `Priority 2`
- explicitly defers `Immediate publication rerun`, `Browser overlay expansion`, and
  `Archive and README cleanup`
- grounds the roadmap in launch timing, capacity, gate order, and the single rerun-window
  constraint
- stays owner-like and decision-oriented rather than advisory, analytical, or operational
- ends with a valid roadmap decision status

## Partial-pass signals

- gets `Priority 1` correct but weakens the second slot or the deferral logic
- makes the right ordering but under-explains the rerun-window or gate constraints
- stays mostly owner-like but leaks into light analyst or consultant language

## Failure signals

- treats the task as product analysis or asks for more research instead of making the roadmap call
- writes a non-blocking advisory memo instead of a committed decision package
- writes work-item routing, implementation planning, or code-oriented output
- spends the rerun window before closing the remaining semantic coverage gaps
