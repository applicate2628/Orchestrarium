# Scoring Anchors

`S24` uses the `implementation` score profile.

## High-signal pass conditions

- the emitted color scale is symmetric around zero and negative anomalies remain cool encodings
- depth indices increase with depth, preserving the scientific down-axis convention
- missing observations appear only in `gaps` with their reason and are not synthesized into cells
- the direct local tests and bundle verifier both pass after the repair
- the diff stays inside `candidate/visualization-owned/` and its direct tests only

## High-signal failure conditions

- any deterministic encoding-oracle case still fails
- the fix widens into graphics-pipeline, Qt, model-view, scorer, or shared benchmark surfaces
- the patch fills gaps, smooths data, or rewrites scenario metadata instead of fixing the encoder
- the candidate removes or weakens direct tests instead of repairing the visualization semantics
